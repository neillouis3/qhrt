QHRT kanban — features
Paste each block as a Notion card. First line = title.
Suggested columns: Backlog / Doing / Review / Done

---
Recommended next step
After onboarding, Home should suggest one thing: a session, a journey, or a short practice. Not a menu of five products.

---
Daily ritual
Morning and evening versions of the check-in. Mood, one sentence, optional breathe. Builds a reason to open the app besides “do a program.”

---
Mood over time
Turn diary moods and home check-ins into a simple week/month view. People want to see the shape of how they have been, not just today’s note.

---
Insights from what you already did
After a few sessions, show patterns in plain language: you finish sleep work more than body work, evenings are heavier, you skip mid-journey, etc.

---
More than sleep
A second and third program people can actually finish. Anxiety/activation, body tension, grief, or low mood — whatever the content team can stand behind.

---
Micro-practices
Two-to-five minute tools next to full sessions: grounding, body scan, 5-4-3-2-1, a shorter breathe, a single prompt. For when they will not start a 15-minute session.

---
More breathing patterns
Box, 4-7-8, longer exhale, count-only. Let people pick a pattern and save a default.

---
Sound
Optional quiet audio under breathe and wind-down: rain, room tone, a simple tone. Mute by default. No stock spa playlist dumped on people.

---
Guided audio sessions
Read the session prompts aloud (or record them) so someone can close their eyes. Especially for sleep and body work.

---
Wind-down for night
A dedicated evening path: dimmer UI, slower breathe, one reflection, lights-out. Separate from “do a therapeutic engine at 11pm.”

---
Morning intention
One prompt when they open the app before noon. Not a new product — a thin layer on Home.

---
Journal prompts
Diary can offer a question of the day, or a prompt tied to the last session, instead of a blank page.

---
Session recap
When a session ends, a one-screen recap: what you covered, what you wrote, optional save-to-diary. Then Home.

---
Homework between sessions
A small thing to try before the next visit: notice one sensation, one evening without the phone in bed, etc. Shows up on Home until they mark it done.

---
Favorites and continue later
Star a practice. Resume is already there — make it obvious, and let people pin the ones they actually repeat.

---
Programs as weeks
Package a journey as “week 1–4” with a suggested pace and no shame if they drift. Progress that looks like a path, not a database of phases.

---
Goals they set themselves
“Sleep through the night more often,” “less jaw tension,” written in their words. Tie sessions to a goal so Home has a north star.

---
Reminders
Optional email or push: time they chose, once a day max. Easy to turn off. Never nag after they skip.

---
Streaks, gently
A quiet “you showed up 4 days this week.” No fire emojis, no losing a streak as punishment.

---
Weekly letter to yourself
Sunday (or their day) recap: moods, sessions done, one line they wrote. Exportable. Feels like a product, not an analytics dump.

---
French
Canada. At least UI + a first program in FR. Language in Settings.

---
Install as an app
PWA so it sits on the phone home screen. Offline for breathe + last diary draft would be a bonus later.

---
Crisis resources by region
Not a crisis product, but a short list: local emergency, provincial mental-health lines, 988 where it applies. Link from Home and session chrome.

---
Share with a clinician
Optional export or invite-only view: “here is what I have been working on.” User owns the switch. Default private.

---
Practitioner accounts
A therapist or coach assigns a program, sees completion (not diary unless the user shares). Different from admin content editing.

---
Assigned path
Admin/practitioner picks the next assessment or journey for a person so they are not staring at three catalogs.

---
Search and library
One library of practices, sessions, and journeys with search. Nav stays short; the catalog lives in one place.

---
Custom session length
Short / regular / unhurried. Same content, fewer prompts or more space.

---
Notifications that respect night
No pings after a quiet-hours window they set.

---
Onboarding that routes you
A few questions, then “start here” instead of dumping every tool on day one.

---
Revisit a finished session
Read-only recap of answers without starting over. “What did I say that week.”

---
Body-first practices
Scan, tension release, posture check — the physical side of QHRT as first-class, not only talk prompts.

---
Sleep toolkit
Assessment + session + wind-down + optional diary tag for nights. One hub, not four separate pages.

---
Accessibility
Larger type, reduced motion already started, screen reader pass on session prompts, captions if audio ships.

---
Community of one
No social feed. If you ever do “you’re not the only one,” keep it anonymous stats (“other people also work on sleep”) not comments.

---
Research opt-in
Anonymized, separate from care, easy no. Only if someone actually wants the data.

---
Waitlist / invite
If you need to slow signups: invite codes, or a landing waitlist, without blocking the current intern demo.

---
More public story
Landing/about that explains the method in human language (mind, body, emotion) and what a first week looks like. Screenshots of the calm UI, not admin tables.

---
Account that feels finished
Forgot password, change password, delete account, download my data. Not flashy — table stakes so people trust a mental-health app.

---
i18n-ready content format
So a second language or a second program is not a rewrite of every prompt component.
